# On your phone

Open the same URL as on your PC in your phone's browser. No app to install.

## Opening

- Open the same URL as on your PC in your phone's browser. The phone screen appears automatically.
- Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

## The screen

Switch with the four icons at the bottom.

| Icon | Contents |
|---|---|
| House (home) | Search, recently opened pages, favorites, folders, collections, tags |
| Documents | All docs |
| Calendar | Journals (pages by date) |
| Pencil | Create a new page |

The bell at the top right opens notifications, and the gear opens settings.

## Writing

- Tap the body to write. The `/` menu works too.
- Select text to show the formatting menu, such as bold.

## Differences from the PC

- Edgeless (the whiteboard) can be viewed but is not suited to editing.
- Use the PC for detailed settings.
- The content is the same as on the PC. Whichever you write on, it reaches the other right away.
