# Handy features

## Search

- Use **Quick search** (Ctrl+K) in the left sidebar to search across page titles and bodies.
- Japanese search is supported. Even a half-remembered phrase can find the page.
- **Pages you cannot read never appear in search results.** Not even their titles, so you cannot tell they exist.
- The same window also lets you create pages and change settings.

## Backlinks

When another page links to a page with `@`, the linked page lists it at the bottom under **Bi-Directional Links**.
You can later trace "which meeting notes refer to this procedure". The list updates automatically.

## Mentions and notifications

- Pick a member with `@` in the body or a comment, and they get a notification.
- See your notifications under **Notifications** in the left sidebar.

## Templates

- Turn a page layout you use often into a template, and start new pages from it.
- Useful for documents that should share a format, such as meeting notes, daily reports, and specifications.

## Protected mode (prevent accidental edits)

For pages that are mostly read, turn on protected mode to avoid changing them by accident.

1. Open **Info** under the page title and check **Protected** at the top.
2. Even people who can edit open the page as read-only. A banner appears at the top.
3. To edit, turn it off in the same place.

Protected mode is not a permission. Anyone who can edit can turn it off. To control who can read or edit, use [Sharing and permissions](./sharing.md).

## Import and export

| Action | How | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Import | **Import** in the left sidebar | Markdown (including zip files with images), HTML, Notion exports |
| Export | The page's **…** → **Export** | PDF, Markdown, HTML, and more |
| Print | **Export** → **Print** | |

## Editing together and offline

- Several people can write in the same page at once. Changes reach everyone immediately.
- What you write while offline is merged automatically when you reconnect.

## Trash and restore

- Deleted pages go to the trash, and you can restore them from there.
- ⚠️ **Delete permanently in the trash cannot be undone.**
- You can also go back to earlier content (the page's **…** → **View history version**).
