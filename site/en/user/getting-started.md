# Getting started

## What is ofuro-wiki

ofuro-wiki is a wiki for your organization. You build pages out of "blocks", and several people can edit the same page at once.
Meeting notes, procedures, specifications, FAQs — keep all of your internal documents in one place.

## Signing in

1. Open the URL your administrator gave you.
2. Enter your email address and press **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and sign in.

- If no account exists for that email address, a **Create account** confirmation appears after you enter the password. If you mistyped the address, press **Back to sign in**.
  On servers that do not accept new registrations, your administrator creates your account.
- If you received an invitation email or link, open it from there. You can create your account and join in one step.
- If single sign-on (SSO) is set up, a button for it appears on the sign-in screen.

You stay signed in for 30 days.

## The screen

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| Left sidebar | Switch workspaces, search, All docs, Journals, Settings, Favorites, folders, Trash, and **User guide** (this manual) |
| Center | The open page. Read and write here |
| Top right | Actions for the open page, such as **Share** and info (ⓘ) |

## Workspaces

Pages are grouped into "workspaces". Click the workspace name at the top left to switch.

| Kind | Who uses it | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Private** | Only you (one per person, created automatically on your first sign-in) | Drafts and personal notes |
| **Shared** | People who were invited (created by an administrator) | The company wiki, department wikis |

- You cannot invite anyone to a private workspace. Administrators cannot open it either.
- Ask an administrator to create a shared workspace and invite you.

## It works on your phone

Open the same URL in your phone's browser and you get a phone-friendly screen. No app to install.
See [On your phone](./mobile) for details.
