features:
  - title: Block editor
    details: Place tables and columns with slash commands and drag & drop.<br>Several people can edit at the same time.
  - title: Confidential pages
    details: A page shared with only some people never appears in anyone else's list or search results. Not even its title leaks.
  - title: Nothing sent outside
    details: No telemetry, no usage statistics. On your LAN or in the cloud, ofuro-wiki sends no data outside.
  - title: Runs on Docker
    details: One Linux server and Docker are all you need. It also runs on a Windows PC.
  - title: Full-text search
    details: Searches page bodies, not just titles, with strong Japanese support.<br>Pages you cannot read never show up.
  - title: Private and shared
    details: Drafts go in your own private workspace. The company wiki lives in shared workspaces your admins set up.
  - title: Audit log
    details: Records who did what and when — including folder changes, and optionally who opened which page.
  - title: Works with AI agents
    details: AI agents such as Claude Code can search, read, and write pages within their permissions.
